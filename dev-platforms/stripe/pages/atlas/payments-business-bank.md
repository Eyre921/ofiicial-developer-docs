# Business bank accounts

Learn about business account options after incorporating with Atlas.

After you incorporate, check whether Stripe Treasury is available for your Stripe account. If you’re eligible, you can apply directly from the business banking section of the Atlas Dashboard.

## Stripe Treasury 

- If eligible, apply immediately after incorporation, before you receive your EIN.
- Use [Stripe Treasury](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury.md) to store, send, and spend funds, manage supported fiat and stablecoin balances, create cards, manage expenses, and access financing.

## Other financial providers

If Stripe Treasury isn’t available for your account, you can apply directly to a third-party financial provider. Read [How to get a US bank account](https://support.stripe.com/questions/how-to-get-a-us-bank-account) for guidance on choosing a provider and applying for an account.
