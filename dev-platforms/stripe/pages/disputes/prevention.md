# Understand fraud

Learn how to identify fraud, and design a strategy to prevent it.

Stripe provides resources and tools to help you develop a strategy for detecting and preventing fraud and other types of disputed payments.

[Types of fraud](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/prevention/fraud-types.md): Explore common types of fraud and strategies to prevent each type.

[Card testing](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/prevention/card-testing.md): Monitor decline activity to identify and prevent card testing.

[Identify fraud](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/prevention/identifying-fraud.md): Recognize common fraud indicators and implement safeguards against them.

[Best practices](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/prevention/best-practices.md): Prevent fraud with clear customer communication, comprehensive customer data, and multi-factor authentication.
