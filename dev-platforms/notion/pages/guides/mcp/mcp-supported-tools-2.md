Explore Notion MCP tools to manage projects and tasks, find meeting notes, share feedback, use Skills, and delegate work to Custom Agents.

Use Notion MCP to manage projects, prepare release updates, and organize marketing content from your AI assistant. A task can combine several tools: find the relevant pages, read their content, create a draft, and update a tracker.

Explore the categories below. Each detailed page includes the available tools and a suggested prompt to try in your AI assistant.

> [card] [Search and read information](/guides/mcp/tools-search)
>
> Find answers across Notion and connected sources, summarize meeting notes, and find teammates and teamspaces.

> [card] [Work together in Notion](/guides/mcp/tools-comments)
>
> Track changes, leave comments, and suggest edits that you, teammates, or AI assistants can review later.

> [card] [Create and organize pages](/guides/mcp/tools-pages)
>
> Draft documents, update existing content, and organize pages for your projects and team.

> [card] [Manage databases and views](/guides/mcp/tools-databases)
>
> Create trackers and content calendars, update properties, and organize work with views.

> [card] [Work with files](/guides/mcp/tools-files)
>
> Upload documents, organize files in folders, and get download links.

> [card] [Reuse instructions and delegate work](/guides/mcp/tools-skills-agents)
>
> Save reusable instructions as Skills in Notion, or hand tasks to Custom Agents. Share both with teammates across your workspace. Save and recall your private memories.

> [card] [Manage your time](/guides/mcp/tools-calendars)
>
> Plan your day, find time to meet, and prepare for meetings with connected calendars and Notion notes.

> [card] [FAQs](/guides/mcp/tools-faqs)
>
> Find answers about tool access, client differences, and rate limits.
