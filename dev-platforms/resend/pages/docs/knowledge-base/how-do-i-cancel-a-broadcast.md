Stop a Broadcast that is still sending and cancel all queued deliveries.

You can cancel a Broadcast while it is still sending. Canceling stops the send and cancels all queued deliveries, so no further emails go out.

## Cancel a Broadcast

1. Go to [Broadcasts](https://resend.com/broadcasts) and open the Broadcast that is sending.
2. Click **Cancel** on the Broadcast page.

Resend then halts delivery to the remaining recipients.

<Note>
  Canceling is only available while a Broadcast is still sending (`queued`). It
  stops delivery to recipients who haven't received the email yet. Emails that
  have already been sent cannot be recalled.
</Note>
