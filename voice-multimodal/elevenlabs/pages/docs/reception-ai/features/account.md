# Account

**Settings** → **Account** is about your own login. It does not change your receptionist, your business profile, or your plan.

## Email and password

The section is titled **Email & Password** when you sign in with an email and password, and **Email** when you sign in another way. It shows **Email Address**.

Select **Change Password** to email yourself a reset link. When it sends, the dashboard shows **Password reset email sent. Check your inbox.**

If you sign in with Google, Apple, or SSO, that name is shown instead, and there is no password to change.

## Two-factor authentication

**Two-Factor Authentication** adds an authenticator app. The badge reads **Enabled** or **Disabled**.

Select **Enable 2FA**. If your email is not confirmed yet, the dialog is titled **Verify your email**: Reception.ai sends a link, and you select **Retry** after you open it. Otherwise the dialog is **Add Two-Factor Authentication**. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup code by hand, then enter the **Verification Code** and select **Verify & Enable**. Save the **Backup Code** that follows. You need it to turn two-factor authentication off, and to sign in if you lose the authenticator app.

Select **Remove 2FA** to open **Remove Two-Factor Authentication**. Enter the backup code under **Your Backup Code**, then select **Remove 2FA**.

## Application language

**Application language** sets the language of the dashboard. It does not change the language your receptionist speaks. See [Voice and personality](/docs/reception-ai/receptionist/voice-and-personality#languages) for that.

The choices are English, Deutsch, Español, Français, हिन्दी, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, Nederlands, Polski, Português, Svenska, 简体中文, and 繁體中文.

## Sign out of every device

**Sign Out All Devices** signs you out everywhere, including this browser. Confirm with **Sign out**. You will need to sign in again.
