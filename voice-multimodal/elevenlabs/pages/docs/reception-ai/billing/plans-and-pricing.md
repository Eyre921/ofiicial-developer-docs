## Upgrading

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing** and select **Manage Subscription**, or select **Upgrade** in the sidebar.
2. Choose monthly or **Annual (Save 2 months)** billing, then choose a plan.
3. If you have a [referral code](/docs/reception-ai/billing/referral-program), enter it and select **Validate**.
4. Complete payment. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment.

Your plan activates immediately. When upgrading mid-cycle, your remaining credits carry over, up to the new plan's pool.

## Downgrading

When you downgrade to a lower plan:

* Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period.
* Resources beyond the new plan's limits are deactivated, such as extra phone numbers and locations.
* To keep your current plan, select **Undo downgrade** before it takes effect.

## Cancellation

To cancel, go to **Settings** → **Billing**, select **Manage Subscription**, and select **Cancel plan**. You'll be asked why you're cancelling. Monthly subscribers may be offered 50% off their next month.

After cancelling:

* Service continues until the end of your billing period.
* Your phone numbers are released after a 14-day grace period.
* Your data is kept in case you resubscribe.

Select **Renew subscription** before the period ends to undo the cancellation.

> **Warning**
>
> Cancelling releases your phone numbers after the grace period. You may get different numbers if
> you resubscribe.

## Payment issues

If a payment fails, a banner asks you to update your payment method. If the billing period ends without payment, your receptionist is paused and the dashboard shows **Your payment didn't go through**. Select **Update payment method** to restore service.
