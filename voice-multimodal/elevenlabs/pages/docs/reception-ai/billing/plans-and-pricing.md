# Plans and pricing

Reception.ai offers a free trial and three paid plans. You can upgrade at any time.

Prices are in USD and exclude tax. All plans include automatic booking, call summaries, transcripts, and access to thousands of voices in over 70 languages.

## Free trial

Every new workspace can start a **14-day free trial** that includes:

* 30 credits (up to 30 phone minutes or 60 web minutes)
* A phone number
* Access to all features and integrations, except custom voices
* No credit card required

Select **Start free trial** at the end of onboarding or on the subscription screen. Each workspace can use one trial. Trial credits are a hard cap: when they run out, your receptionist stops answering until you upgrade. See [Usage and limits](/docs/reception-ai/billing/usage-and-limits#low-credit-warnings).

If you are waiting on a [regulatory request](/docs/reception-ai/phone-numbers/international-numbers) for a phone number, Reception.ai keeps at least 14 days left on your trial while the request is in review, and emails you when it extends your trial. Only the end date moves: your trial credits don't refill.

## How credits work

Each plan includes a monthly credit pool shared across all activity:

| Activity                                | Credits         |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Inbound phone call                      | 1.0 per minute  |
| Web widget or booking page conversation | 0.5 per minute  |
| Staff first call                        | 1.0 per minute  |
| Text message                            | 0.1 per message |

## Overage

On paid plans, usage beyond your included credits is billed at your plan's overage rate. A phone minute is one credit: Basic **$0.40**, Plus **$0.32**, and Premium **\$0.22** per extra phone minute. Web minutes and text messages draw from the same pool, so they are billed at that rate per credit.

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing** and select **Manage Subscription**, or select **Upgrade** in the sidebar.
2. Choose monthly or **Annual (Save 2 months)** billing, then choose a plan.
3. If you have a [referral code](/docs/reception-ai/billing/referral-program), enter it and select **Validate**.
4. Complete payment. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment.

Your plan activates immediately. When upgrading mid-cycle, your remaining credits carry over, up to the new plan's pool.

## Downgrading

When you downgrade to a lower plan:

* Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period.
* Resources beyond the new plan's limits are deactivated, such as extra phone numbers and locations.
* Team members beyond the new plan's limit are suspended, not removed. See [Team](/docs/reception-ai/features/team#suspended-members).
* To keep your current plan, select **Undo downgrade** before it takes effect.

## Cancellation

To cancel, go to **Settings** → **Billing**, select **Manage Subscription**, and select **Cancel plan**. You'll be asked why you're cancelling. Monthly subscribers may be offered 50% off their next month.

After cancelling:

* Service continues until the end of your billing period.
* Your phone numbers are released after a 14-day grace period.
* Your data is kept in case you resubscribe.

Select **Renew subscription** before the period ends to undo the cancellation.

> **Warning**
>
> Cancelling releases your phone numbers after the grace period. You may get different numbers if
> you resubscribe.

## Payment issues

If a payment fails, a banner asks you to update your payment method. If the billing period ends without payment, your receptionist is paused and the dashboard shows **Your payment didn't go through**. Select **Update payment method** to restore service.
