# Is there a limit on how many times I can edit / add voices?

Yes, depending on your subscription tier, there are different monthly limits on how many voice operations you can make (creating a new voice or editing an existing voice).

Your operations are reset when you get new credits. For most paid plans, this is monthly. Grant recipients have 12 times the number of edits as Business tier subscriptions, but these reset once per year.

* Free: 55
* Starter: 65
* Creator: 95
* Pro: 290
* Scale: 1040
* Business: 1040
* Enterprise: Unlimited
