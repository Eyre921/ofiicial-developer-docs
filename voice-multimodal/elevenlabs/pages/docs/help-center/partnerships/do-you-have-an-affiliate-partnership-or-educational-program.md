# Do you have an affiliate, partnership or educational program?

At ElevenLabs, we’re committed to making a positive impact on society through the power of voice. Our Impact Program partners with nonprofits to provide free licenses, supporting initiatives that drive social good. To find out more and apply, please visit our [Impact Program page.](https://elevenlabs.io/impact-program)

We're working in partnership with Bridging Voice, The Scott-Morgan Foundation and other organizations to help people who are losing their voice due to medical conditions such as ALS (Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis) or MND (Motor Neurone Disease). If you or a loved one could benefit from this, please visit our [Impact Program page](https://elevenlabs.io/impact-program) to learn more and apply.

ElevenLabs operates an Affiliate program. To find out more, please see our [Affiliates page](https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates). To get started, create an account and log in. Click your profile icon in the top right corner of the website to open your account menu, then select **Affiliate program**.
