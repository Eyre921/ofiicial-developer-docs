# Do you offer discounted or free plans?

We have a free plan that anyone can use, and you will automatically be assigned to this plan when you sign up for an account.

We sometimes offer discounts on the first month of some of our plans, to allow you to test the service with more features not available on the free plan.  You can find out if we currently have any offers available by checking your [Subscription](https://elevenlabs.io/app/subscription) page.

If you are a non-profit or education institution, please visit our [Impact Program page](https://elevenlabs.io/impact-program), and complete the form to get in touch.

If you're an individual with permanent speech loss caused by a condition such as ALS, head and neck cancer, cerebral palsy or PSP, you can apply for a free subscription directly on our website through our Impact Program partners. For an overview of how to apply, including a video showing the process, see this [blog post.](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/expanding-access-patients-and-clinicians-can-now-apply-directly-on-the-elevenlabs-website)
