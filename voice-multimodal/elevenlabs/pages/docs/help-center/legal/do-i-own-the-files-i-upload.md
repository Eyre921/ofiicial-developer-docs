# Do I own the files I upload?

**We claim no rights** over the files you upload to the platform, except the rights
outlined in the [Terms of Service, ](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use)[Privacy
Policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy) and [Data Processing
Addendum](https://elevenlabs.io/dpa), and to review said files in accordance with local laws.

If you wish to opt out of specific data usage as described in our [Terms of Service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) and [Privacy Policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy), please follow the instructions in this article: [Is my data used to improve ElevenLabs’ AI models?](/docs/help-center/legal/is-my-data-used-to-improve-eleven-labs-ai-models)
