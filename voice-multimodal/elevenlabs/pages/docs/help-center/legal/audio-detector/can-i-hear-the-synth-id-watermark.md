# Can I hear the SynthID watermark?

No. The watermark is completely inaudible. SynthID hides a pattern of sound at frequencies that are masked by the existing audio, making it imperceptible to the human ear.

For more information, see [What is SynthID, and why is ElevenLabs using it?](/docs/help-center/legal/audio-detector/what-is-synth-id-and-why-is-eleven-labs-using-it)
