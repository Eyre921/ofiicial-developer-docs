# Will watermarks cause my content to be flagged, downranked, or demonetized on YouTube or other platforms?

No other platforms can currently detect ElevenLabs watermarks. Our watermarking is focused on public AI transparency — giving people the ability to verify whether audio is AI-generated — and is not connected to other platforms’ monetization or content policies.

For more information, see [What is watermarking, and why is ElevenLabs using it?](/docs/help-center/legal/audio-detector/what-is-watermarking-and-why-is-eleven-labs-using-it)
