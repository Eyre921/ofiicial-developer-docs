# Is content generated by third-party models available through ElevenLabs watermarked?

Generally no. Our watermarking is specific to audio content that is generated directly by ElevenLabs. Content generated by third-party models available through the platform is typically not watermarked unless explicitly stated otherwise.

For more information, see [What is watermarking, and why is ElevenLabs using it?](/docs/help-center/legal/audio-detector/what-is-watermarking-and-why-is-eleven-labs-using-it)
