# Does ElevenLabs have an official app?

Yes, ElevenLabs offers two native mobile apps for iOS, iPadOS, and Android users. Both apps support 32 languages and are available worldwide.

* [Download for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/elevenreader-text-to-audio/id6479373050?platform=iphone) - Requires iOS 17.0 or later.
* [Download for iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/elevenreader-text-to-audio/id6479373050?platform=ipad) - Requires iPadOS 17.0 or later.
* [Download for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.elevenlabs.readerapp) - Requires Android 7.0 or later.
