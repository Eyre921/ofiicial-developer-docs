# How can I contact support?

For general inquiries, you can use the AI chatbot in the bottom right of the help center and documentation, powered by our ElevenAgents platform. It's trained on our knowledge base and can answer many common questions.

For specific questions that require direct support, email us at [team@elevenlabs.io](mailto:team@elevenlabs.io). Please include as much detail as possible, along with any screenshots, history/request IDs, studio/project IDs, or audio examples of the issue, and email us from your ElevenLabs account email address. This will help us resolve your inquiry quickly.
