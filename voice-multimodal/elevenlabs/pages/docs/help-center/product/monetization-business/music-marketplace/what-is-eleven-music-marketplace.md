# What is Eleven Music Marketplace?

Eleven Music Marketplace is a library of AI-generated songs created by ElevenLabs users and available for licensing across different usage types. Creators can publish songs generated with ElevenLabs, and other users can purchase usage rights to use, remix, and download those songs for their own projects.

When a song is purchased, the buyer receives a license based on the selected [usage type](/docs/help-center/product/monetization-business/music-marketplace/what-usage-types-exist-in-music-marketplace), and the [creator receives](/docs/help-center/product/monetization-business/music-marketplace/how-do-creators-earn-money-on-music-marketplace) a portion of the payment as a payout.

Eleven Music Marketplace is designed to make discovering, licensing, remixing, and using music simple and straightforward.
