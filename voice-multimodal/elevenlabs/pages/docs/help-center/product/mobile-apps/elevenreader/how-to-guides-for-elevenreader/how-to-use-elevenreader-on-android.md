# How to use ElevenReader on Android

ElevenReader is a text-to-speech app that lets you listen to books, articles, documents, and other written content using AI voices.

You can download ElevenReader on your Android phone or tablet, import your own content, or explore the reading materials available in the app.

### How do I download ElevenReader on Android?

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android device.
2. Search for **ElevenReader**.
3. Select the [ElevenReader app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.elevenlabs.readerapp\&hl=en) from ElevenLabs.
4. Tap **Install**.
5. Open ElevenReader and follow the on-screen instructions.

ElevenReader requires Android 7.0 or later. Devices with rooted operating systems or unlocked bootloaders are not supported, because of security restrictions.

If the Google Play Store says your device is incompatible, check your Android version and device configuration.

For more information, see [Which devices is ElevenReader available on?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/which-devices-is-elevenreader-available-on).

### How do I add content to ElevenReader on Android?

1. Open ElevenReader on your Android device.
2. Tap **+**.
3. Choose how you want to add content:
   * **Write text.** Type or paste text directly into ElevenReader.
   * **Scan text.** Use your device's camera to capture printed text.
   * **Paste a link.** Import content from a supported webpage.
   * **Upload a file.** Select a document saved on your Android device.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to add the content.
5. Open the imported content and press play.

ElevenReader supports PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT, and HTML files.

For more information, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).

### How do I choose a voice in ElevenReader?

1. Open the book, article, or document you want to listen to.
2. Open the voice options in the player.
3. Browse the available AI voices.
4. Select the voice you want to use.
5. Press play.

You can try different voices to find one that suits your listening preferences.

### How do I change the playback speed?

1. Open the content you want to listen to.
2. Open the playback controls.
3. Find the playback speed setting.
4. Choose your preferred listening speed.
5. Continue listening at the selected speed.

You can change the speed again at any time.

### How do I listen to websites on Android?

**Open a webpage** lets you browse supported websites and listen to their content directly.

1. Open ElevenReader on your Android device.
2. Tap **+** and select **Open a webpage**.
3. Go to the website you want to listen to.
4. Sign in if the website requires an account.
5. Open the article, document, or other supported content.
6. Press play.

ElevenReader supports several online sources, including Google Docs, Substack, Wikipedia, and supported Kindle ebooks.

Content opened with **Open a webpage** is not saved to your ElevenReader library.

For more information, see [How does “Open a webpage” work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-does-open-a-webpage-work).

### Can I listen to content offline on Android?

Yes. Eligible paid subscribers can download supported content for offline listening.

Once the audio has been prepared and downloaded, you can listen without an active Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.

Offline downloads usually take around 2-5 minutes to prepare, although larger documents may take longer.

Downloaded content remains available for 60 days and can be renewed before it expires.

For more information, see [How do offline downloads work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-offline-downloads-work).

### What if ElevenReader isn't working on my Android device?

1. Check your Android version. ElevenReader requires Android 7.0 or later.
2. Check your internet connection. Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data when you import content.
3. Restart the app. Close ElevenReader and open it again.
4. Check for updates. Open the Google Play Store and install any available ElevenReader updates.
5. Try different content. If one document is not working, open another file or article to see whether the problem is specific to that content.
6. Check device compatibility. Rooted devices and phones with unlocked bootloaders are not supported.

If the problem continues, [contact Support](/docs/help-center/other/how-can-i-contact-support). Include a description of the issue, your Android device model, and your Android version.
