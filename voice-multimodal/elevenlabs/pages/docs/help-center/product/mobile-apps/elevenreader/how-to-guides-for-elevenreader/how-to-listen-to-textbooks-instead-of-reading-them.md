# How to listen to textbooks instead of reading them

You can use ElevenReader to listen to digital textbooks and other study materials using AI voices. Upload a textbook in a supported format, such as PDF or EPUB, or listen to supported ebooks online.

You can also scan pages from a printed textbook to turn the text into audio.

### How do I listen to a digital textbook with ElevenReader?

If your textbook is saved as a PDF or EPUB file, upload it to ElevenReader:

1. Open ElevenReader. Launch the app on your iPhone or Android device, or visit [elevenreader.io](https://elevenreader.io/) to use the web version.
2. Select **Upload a file**.
3. Select the PDF or EPUB file you want to listen to.
4. Open the textbook once it has been added to your library.
5. Press play to hear it read aloud.

You can choose an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

### Can I listen to a physical textbook?

**Scan text** captures text from physical pages with your device's camera.

1. Open ElevenReader on your iPhone or Android device.
2. Select **Scan text**.
3. Point your camera at the textbook page you want to listen to.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to capture the text.
5. Open the scanned content and press play.

For longer textbooks, a digital PDF or EPUB is usually easier than scanning individual pages.

Text recognition may be less reliable when pages contain complex layouts, handwritten annotations, or text embedded in images.

### Can I listen to Kindle textbooks with ElevenReader?

Yes. **Open a webpage** supports Kindle ebooks online, including supported textbooks you already have access to.

1. Open ElevenReader on your mobile device.
2. Tap **+** and select **Open a webpage**.
3. Open Kindle from the supported websites list.
4. Sign in to your Amazon account.
5. Open the textbook you want to listen to.
6. Press play.

You can choose an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

Content opened with **Open a webpage** stays in its original location. It is not imported or saved to your ElevenReader library.

For more information, see [How does “Open a webpage” work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-does-open-a-webpage-work).

### What textbook formats does ElevenReader support?

ElevenReader supports several file formats:

* **PDF (`.pdf`).** Common for digital textbooks, academic publications, and study materials.
* **EPUB (`.epub`).** Used for ebooks and digital publications.
* **DOCX (`.docx`).** Microsoft Word documents that contain study materials.
* **TXT (`.txt`).** Plain-text documents.
* **HTML (`.html`).** Text saved as HTML.

The file must be accessible and in a supported format. Some ebooks are protected by digital rights management (DRM), which can prevent a direct upload.

If you have access to a supported online ebook, you may be able to listen to it through **Open a webpage** instead.

For more information, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).

### Can ElevenReader read equations, diagrams, and tables in textbooks?

ElevenReader converts written text into audio. Some textbook content is difficult to follow without the original page:

* **Mathematical equations.** Complex formulas and mathematical notation may not be pronounced as intended.
* **Diagrams.** Illustrations and labeled figures may need to be viewed in the original textbook.
* **Tables.** Information in rows and columns may lose its structure when read aloud.
* **Charts and graphs.** Visual relationships between data points may not come through in audio.
* **Special characters.** Scientific symbols, abbreviations, and unusual notation may not always be pronounced correctly.

For textbooks containing these elements, we recommend referring to the original pages alongside the audio.

### Can I listen to textbooks offline?

Yes. Eligible paid subscribers can download supported textbooks and other documents for offline listening.

You can prepare an entire document or, for larger files, select individual chapters or sections.

Offline downloads usually take around 2-5 minutes to prepare, although larger files may take longer.

Once downloaded, the content plays without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Downloads remain available for 60 days and can be renewed before they expire.

For more information, see [How do offline downloads work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-offline-downloads-work).

### What if my textbook won't upload?

1. Check the file format. Confirm that the textbook is a PDF, EPUB, or another supported format.
2. Check that the file opens. Open the textbook on your device to confirm that the file is not damaged.
3. Check for access restrictions. Some ebooks use DRM that prevents a direct upload.
4. Check your internet connection. Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
5. Try uploading the file again. Reopen ElevenReader and select **Upload a file**.

If the textbook is available on a supported website, try listening through **Open a webpage** instead.
