# How to turn study notes into audio

You can use ElevenReader to turn study notes into audio using AI voices. Upload a document, paste notes into the app, or scan printed pages and listen to them aloud.

ElevenReader supports PDF, DOCX, and TXT files, so you can listen to notes saved on your phone, tablet, or computer.

### How do I turn study notes into audio with ElevenReader?

If your notes are saved as a document, upload them to ElevenReader:

1. Open ElevenReader. Launch the app on your iPhone or Android device, or visit [elevenreader.io](https://elevenreader.io/) to use the web version.
2. Select **Upload a file**.
3. Select the PDF, DOCX, or TXT file you want to listen to.
4. Open the file once it has been added to your library.
5. Press play to hear your notes read aloud.

You can also choose an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

### Can I turn typed study notes into audio without uploading a file?

Yes. **Write text** lets you type or paste text directly into the app.

1. Open ElevenReader.
2. Select **Write text**.
3. Type or paste the study notes you want to listen to.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to add the text.
5. Open the content and press play.

This option works well for shorter notes, revision summaries, or text copied from another document.

### How do I listen to printed study notes?

**Scan text** captures printed notes with your device's camera.

1. Open ElevenReader on your iPhone or Android device.
2. Select **Scan text**.
3. Point your camera at the printed page.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to scan the text.
5. Open the captured content and start listening.

For the most reliable results, make sure the text is clearly visible and the page is well lit.

Handwritten notes may not be recognized reliably, especially when the handwriting is difficult to read. If a scan does not capture the text correctly, type the notes into ElevenReader instead.

### What file formats can I use for study notes?

ElevenReader supports these file formats:

* **PDF (`.pdf`).** Study guides, lecture notes, and exported documents.
* **DOCX (`.docx`).** Notes created in Microsoft Word or another compatible application.
* **TXT (`.txt`).** Plain-text notes.
* **HTML (`.html`).** Notes saved as HTML.
* **EPUB (`.epub`).** Ebooks and other digital reading materials.

If your notes are in an unsupported format, export them as a PDF or DOCX file before you upload them.

For more information, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).

### Can ElevenReader read notes containing equations, diagrams, and tables?

ElevenReader converts written text into speech. Some study content is difficult to follow as audio:

* **Mathematical equations.** Complex formulas and mathematical notation may not be pronounced as intended.
* **Diagrams.** Visual information may need to be viewed in the original document.
* **Tables.** Information in rows and columns may lose its original structure when read aloud.
* **Charts and graphs.** Visual relationships between data points may not come through in audio.
* **Special characters.** Scientific symbols and abbreviations may not always be pronounced correctly.

For notes containing complex visual information, we recommend keeping the original document available while listening.

### What if my study notes won't upload?

1. Check the file format. Confirm that the notes are saved as PDF, DOCX, TXT, or another supported format.
2. Check that the file opens. Open the document on your device to confirm that it is not damaged.
3. Check your internet connection. Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
4. Try uploading the file again. Close and reopen ElevenReader, then select **Upload a file**.
5. Try another format. If the document is not importing, export it as a PDF, or copy the text into **Write text**.

For printed notes, you can try scanning the pages again, or enter the text manually.
