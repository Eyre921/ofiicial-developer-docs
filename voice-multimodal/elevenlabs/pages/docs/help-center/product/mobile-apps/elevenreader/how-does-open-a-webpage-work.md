# How does “Open a webpage” work?

ElevenReader's **Open a webpage** feature lets you listen to websites and online content you already have access to using ElevenLabs voices.

In ElevenReader, tap **+** > **Open a webpage**, browse to the site you want to listen to, open your content, and press play. You can choose from more than 1,000 voices and adjust the playback speed.

The feature works with public webpages and supported sites that require you to sign in, including Kindle eBooks online, Google Docs, Substack, X, and popular web novel sites.

### Supported websites and content

ElevenReader can read supported Kindle eBooks online, Google Docs, X articles and threads, Substack newsletters, web novels, Wikipedia articles, and other webpages aloud.

Tap **+** > **Open a webpage**, navigate to content you already have access to, and press play.

Supported sites and content types include:

* Kindle eBooks online
* Google Docs
* X articles and threads
* Substack newsletters
* Popular web novel sites
* News and articles
* Blogs and other webpages

Check the in-app list for the current supported sites and content types.

### Websites that require sign-in

ElevenReader can read content from supported websites that require you to sign in. You must already have access to the content.

For example, you can sign in to Kindle in the ElevenReader browser, open an eBook you have access to, and listen with your preferred ElevenLabs voice.

### Mispronunciations

ElevenReader reads content directly from the webpage. Formatting such as unusual spacing, abbreviations, symbols, or special characters can cause mispronunciations.

If you find an example, [contact Support](/docs/help-center/other/how-can-i-contact-support) and include the relevant details.

### Library behavior

**Open a webpage** is intended for private listening to content you already have access to. Content opened this way is not imported, copied, or saved to your ElevenReader library.

When you press play, ElevenReader reads the content from the webpage without creating a separate copy. Your Kindle eBooks, subscriptions, Google Docs, newsletters, articles, and other content remain in their original locations.

To listen again, return to the webpage in ElevenReader and press play.

### Listening allowance

You can use ElevenReader for up to 10 hours of listening per month for free. This is approximately 400 pages of written content.
