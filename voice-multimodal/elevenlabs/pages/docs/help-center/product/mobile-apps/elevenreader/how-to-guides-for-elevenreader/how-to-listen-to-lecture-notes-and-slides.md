# How to listen to lecture notes and slides

You can use ElevenReader to listen to lecture notes, presentations, and other study materials using AI voices. Upload notes as a supported document, or scan printed pages to turn the text into audio.

ElevenReader supports PDF, DOCX, and TXT files, so you can listen to lecture materials on your phone, tablet, or computer.

### How do I listen to lecture notes with ElevenReader?

If your lecture notes are saved as a PDF or Word document, upload them to ElevenReader:

1. Open ElevenReader. Launch the app on your iPhone or Android device, or visit [elevenreader.io](https://elevenreader.io/) to use the web version.
2. Select **Upload a file**.
3. Select the PDF, DOCX, or TXT file you want to listen to.
4. Open the file once it has been imported.
5. Press play to hear your lecture notes read aloud.

You can also choose an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

### How do I listen to PowerPoint slides?

ElevenReader does not currently support PowerPoint files (`.ppt` or `.pptx`) as an upload format.

Instead, you can export the presentation as a PDF, then upload that file:

1. Open the presentation in Microsoft PowerPoint.
2. Select **File**, then **Export** or **Save As**, depending on your version of PowerPoint.
3. Choose **PDF** as the file format.
4. Save the PDF to your device.
5. Open ElevenReader and select **Upload a file**.
6. Choose the exported PDF and open it to start listening.

For presentations that contain detailed diagrams, charts, or images, keep the original slides available so you can refer to the visual content while you listen.

### Can I listen to Google Slides presentations?

Export a Google Slides presentation as a PDF, then upload it to ElevenReader:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.
2. Select **File** > **Download** > **PDF Document (.pdf)**.
3. Save the downloaded PDF to your device.
4. Open ElevenReader and select **Upload a file**.
5. Choose the PDF and press play.

To listen to speaker notes as well, include them in a supported document before you upload it. A standard PDF export of the slides may not include speaker notes.

### Can I listen to handwritten lecture notes?

**Scan text** captures printed text with your device's camera.

1. Open ElevenReader on your iPhone or Android device.
2. Select **Scan text**.
3. Point your camera at the page you want to scan.
4. Capture the text using the on-screen instructions.
5. Open the captured content and start listening.

The feature is intended for printed text. Handwritten notes may not be recognized reliably, especially when the handwriting is difficult to read.

For handwritten materials, consider typing the notes into a document, or converting them into readable text before importing them.

### Can ElevenReader read diagrams, equations, and charts in lecture slides?

ElevenReader converts written text into spoken audio. Visual information may not translate directly into speech:

* **Diagrams.** Illustrations and labeled figures may need to be viewed in the original presentation.
* **Charts and graphs.** Visual relationships between data points may not come through in audio.
* **Mathematical equations.** Complex formulas and mathematical notation may not be pronounced as intended.
* **Tables.** Information in rows and columns may lose its original structure when read aloud.
* **Images.** Text embedded in images may not be recognized in an uploaded document.

If your lecture materials contain these elements, we recommend referring to the original slides or notes alongside the audio.

### What if my lecture notes won't upload?

1. Check the file format. ElevenReader supports PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML, and EPUB files. Convert PowerPoint presentations to a supported format before you upload them.
2. Check that the file opens. Open the document on your device to confirm that it is not damaged.
3. Check your internet connection. Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
4. Try uploading the file again. Close and reopen ElevenReader, then select **Upload a file**.
5. Try a different format. If a document is not importing, export it as a PDF or save the text as a DOCX file.

For more information, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).
