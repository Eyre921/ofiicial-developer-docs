# How to listen to PDFs on Android

You can listen to PDF documents on your Android phone or tablet using ElevenReader. The app converts written text into spoken audio, so you can listen to reports, study materials, ebooks, and other PDF files using AI voices.

### How do I upload a PDF to ElevenReader on Android?

1. Open ElevenReader. Download it from the Google Play Store if you have not already, then open the app on your Android device.
2. Select **Upload a file**.
3. Choose your PDF. Browse files stored on your device or in a connected cloud storage service, such as Google Drive.
4. Import the document. Select the PDF you want to add to ElevenReader.
5. Open your PDF. Once it appears in your library, open it and press play.

You can also select an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

### What types of PDFs can I listen to?

ElevenReader supports PDF documents that contain readable text, including:

* Research papers and academic articles
* Study notes and educational materials
* Reports and business documents
* Guides and manuals
* Ebooks saved as PDFs

For the most reliable listening experience, use PDFs with selectable text. Scanned or image-based documents may need text recognition before they can be read aloud.

If a document contains complex formatting, tables, or diagrams, you may need to refer to the original PDF alongside the audio.

ElevenReader supports file uploads of up to 500 pages. If your PDF is longer, split it into smaller documents before you upload it.

### Can I listen to a PDF while using other apps?

ElevenReader is designed for listening to written content, so you can follow a document without reading every word on the screen.

For example, you might listen to a research paper while reviewing your notes, or play a report while doing another task.

Playback behavior depends on your Android device settings and the current version of the app.

### Can I listen to PDFs offline on Android?

Yes. ElevenReader supports offline downloads for eligible paid subscribers, so you can listen without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

You can prepare a PDF for offline listening in the app. For larger documents, you may need to download individual chapters or sections.

Offline downloads usually take around 2-5 minutes to prepare, although larger files may take longer.

Downloaded content remains available for 60 days and can be renewed before it expires.

For more information, see [How do offline downloads work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-offline-downloads-work).

### What if my PDF won't upload on Android?

1. Check the file format. Confirm that the document is saved as a PDF.
2. Check the file size and length. ElevenReader supports documents of up to 500 pages. Split longer documents into smaller files.
3. Check that the PDF opens. Open it in your device's file manager or PDF viewer to confirm that it is not damaged.
4. Check your internet connection. Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data before you upload.
5. Try uploading the file again. Close and reopen ElevenReader, then select **Upload a file**.
6. Check for app updates. Open the Google Play Store to see whether an updated version of ElevenReader is available.

If the PDF is password-protected, remove the password using an authorized method before you upload it.

You can also import a different PDF to check whether the issue is specific to one document.

### What Android devices support ElevenReader?

ElevenReader is available on supported Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later.

Devices with rooted operating systems or unlocked bootloaders are not supported, because of security restrictions.

If Google Play indicates that your device is incompatible, check your Android version and device configuration.

For more information, see [Which devices is ElevenReader available on?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/which-devices-is-elevenreader-available-on).

### Can I listen to other document formats on Android?

Yes. ElevenReader supports several file formats in addition to PDFs:

* EPUB (`.epub`)
* Word documents (`.docx`)
* Plain text (`.txt`)
* HTML (`.html`)

You can also paste text directly into ElevenReader, scan printed pages with your device's camera, or import content from a webpage.

For more information about supported content and import options, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).
