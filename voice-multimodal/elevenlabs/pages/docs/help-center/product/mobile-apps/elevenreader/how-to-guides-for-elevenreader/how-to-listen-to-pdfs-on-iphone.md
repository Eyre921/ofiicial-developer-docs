# How to listen to PDFs on iPhone

You can listen to PDF documents on your iPhone using ElevenReader. The app converts written text into spoken audio, so you can listen to reports, study materials, articles, and other PDF files using AI voices.

### How do I upload a PDF to ElevenReader on iPhone?

1. Open ElevenReader. Download it from the App Store if you have not already, then open the app on your iPhone.
2. Select **Upload a file**.
3. Choose your PDF. Find the file saved on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or another location available in the iOS file picker.
4. Import the document. Select the PDF to add it to ElevenReader.
5. Open your PDF. Once it is in your library, open it and press play.

You can also choose an AI voice and adjust the playback speed.

### What types of PDFs can I listen to?

ElevenReader supports PDF files, including:

* Research papers and academic articles
* Study notes and educational materials
* Reports and business documents
* Guides and manuals
* Ebooks saved as PDFs

For the most reliable experience, use PDFs that contain readable, selectable text. Scanned documents and image-based PDFs may need text recognition before they can be read aloud.

If a document contains complex formatting, tables, or diagrams, you may need to refer to the original PDF alongside the audio.

ElevenReader supports file uploads of up to 500 pages. If your PDF is longer, split it into smaller documents before you upload it.

### Can I listen to a PDF while using other apps?

ElevenReader is designed for listening to written content, so you can follow a document without reading every word on the screen.

For example, you might listen to a research paper while reviewing your notes, or play a report while doing another task.

Playback behavior depends on your iPhone settings and the current version of the app.

### Can I listen to PDFs offline on iPhone?

Yes. ElevenReader supports offline downloads for eligible paid subscribers, so you can listen without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

You can prepare a PDF for offline listening in the app. For larger documents, you may need to download individual chapters or sections.

Offline downloads usually take around 2-5 minutes to prepare, although larger files may take longer.

Downloaded content remains available for 60 days and can be renewed before it expires.

For more information, see [How do offline downloads work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-offline-downloads-work).

### What if my PDF won't upload?

1. Check the file format. Confirm that the document is saved as a PDF.
2. Check that the file opens. Open it in the Files app to confirm that it is not damaged.
3. Check your internet connection. Make sure your iPhone has a stable connection before you try again.
4. Try uploading the file again. Close and reopen ElevenReader, then select **Upload a file**.
5. Check for app updates. Open the App Store to see whether a newer version of ElevenReader is available.

If the PDF is password-protected, remove the password using an authorized method before you import it.

You can also upload a different PDF to see whether the problem is specific to one document.

### Can I listen to other document formats on iPhone?

Yes. In addition to PDF files, ElevenReader supports:

* EPUB (`.epub`)
* Word documents (`.docx`)
* Plain text (`.txt`)
* HTML (`.html`)

You can also paste text, scan printed pages, or import content from a webpage.

For more information about supported content and import options, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).
