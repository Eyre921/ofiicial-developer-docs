# How to read websites aloud

You can use ElevenReader to listen to websites, articles, blogs, and other online content using AI voices. **Open a webpage** lets you browse supported websites and listen to content directly, without copying or uploading the text.

### How do I listen to a website with ElevenReader?

1. Open ElevenReader on your iPhone or Android device.
2. Tap **+** and select **Open a webpage**.
3. Browse to the website or online content you want to listen to.
4. Open the article, document, or page.
5. Press play. ElevenReader reads the webpage aloud using an AI voice.

You can choose from more than 1,000 voices and adjust the playback speed.

### What websites can ElevenReader read aloud?

ElevenReader supports a range of websites and online content, including:

* News articles and blogs
* Wikipedia articles
* Google Docs
* Substack newsletters
* X articles and threads
* Popular web novel websites
* Supported Kindle ebooks online

Check the list of supported websites and content types in the ElevenReader app.

Some websites may not work as expected because of their layout, access restrictions, or other technical limitations.

### Can ElevenReader read websites that require a login?

Yes. ElevenReader can read content from supported websites that require you to sign in, if you already have access to that content.

For example, you can open Kindle through ElevenReader's built-in browser, sign in to your Amazon account, and listen to supported ebooks in your library.

The same approach works with other supported websites that require authentication. You must have permission to access the content you want to listen to.

### Can I save webpages to my ElevenReader library?

Content opened with **Open a webpage** is not imported or saved to your ElevenReader library.

ElevenReader reads the content directly from the website. The original webpage, document, or ebook stays where it is.

To listen again, return to the webpage in ElevenReader and press play.

To add a webpage as a separate item in your library, try **Paste a link**.

For more information, see [How do I add content to ElevenReader?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-i-add-content-to-elevenreader).

### What if ElevenReader doesn't read a webpage correctly?

Some webpages contain formatting that affects how text is read aloud, including unusual spacing, abbreviations, symbols, and special characters.

If this happens, open the content again, or check whether the website is included in ElevenReader's supported sites.

If a word or phrase is consistently mispronounced, [contact Support](/docs/help-center/other/how-can-i-contact-support) and include the details.

For more information, see [How does “Open a webpage” work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-does-open-a-webpage-work).

### Can I listen to websites for free?

Yes. ElevenReader includes up to 10 hours of free listening per month, which is about 400 pages of written content.

You can use this allowance to listen to supported webpages and other content.

For more information, see [How do ElevenReader hours work?](/docs/help-center/product/mobile-apps/elevenreader/how-to-guides-for-elevenreader/how-do-elevenreader-hours-work).
