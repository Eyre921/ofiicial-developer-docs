# Are there any limits on the types of content I can listen to in ElevenReader?

Yes, content uploaded to ElevenReader for listening is governed by the <a href="https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy">ElevenLabs Prohibited Use Policy</a>. This policy supplements our Terms of Service and applies to your access and use of our services, and is intended to ensure ElevenLabs’ services are not used for illicit purposes.

We use a combination of automated systems, user reports, and human review to assess material and usage that may violate this Policy. For users who violate this Policy, we may remove the violating material, and/or suspend your access to and use of our Services.

Some specific examples of content that may violate this policy include:

* Do not threaten child safety.
* Do not engage in illegal behavior.
* Do not create violent, hateful, or harassing material outside of fictional contexts.

If you believe your account has been incorrectly banned, or your material has been incorrectly removed, you can let us know by emailing [team@elevenlabs.io](mailto:team@elevenlabs.io) from the email you currently use for your ElevenLabs account.
