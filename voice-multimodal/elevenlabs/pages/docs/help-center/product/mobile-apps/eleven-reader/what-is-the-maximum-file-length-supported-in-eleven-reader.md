# What is the maximum file length supported in ElevenReader?

ElevenReader currently supports uploads of files up to 500 pages.

If your document exceeds 500 pages, you’ll need to split it into smaller parts before uploading. Once divided into separate files under the page limit, they can be uploaded and read normally within the app.

This limit applies to large document uploads to ensure stable parsing and playback performance.
