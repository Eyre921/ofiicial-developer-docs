# What does "Unusual activity detected" mean in ElevenReader?

The "Unusual activity detected" message means that ElevenReader's automated safeguards detected account or network usage associated with potential misuse.

These safeguards help prevent misuse, including attempts to create or use multiple accounts to bypass product limits. The message does not necessarily mean that you intentionally violated a policy. Certain network or account usage patterns can also trigger the safeguards.

After unusual activity is detected, access to affected ElevenReader features may be restricted. Bypassing account restrictions and sharing accounts violate the ElevenLabs Terms of Service.

If you believe the restriction was applied incorrectly, email [support@elevenlabs.io](mailto:support@elevenlabs.io). Include relevant details about your recent activity, such as:

* Recent listening sessions
* Devices you used
* Whether you were connected to a VPN

Support may review the account and available usage information, but cannot guarantee that the restriction will be removed. Support also cannot disclose the signals or criteria used by the abuse prevention systems.
