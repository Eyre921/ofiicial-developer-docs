# Which voices can I use in ElevenReader?

The app features hundreds of curated, quality voices produced with ElevenLabs, including our Iconic Voices collection. 

We provide recommended voices based on your language settings, and you can create your own voices from a text prompt using Voice Design within the app. ElevenReader does not support voices you've created yourself using voice cloning. 

Additionally, the app features thousands of audiobooks with human-narrated, premium audiobooks. For select titles, you may be able to select your own narrator where listed on the book’s listing page.
