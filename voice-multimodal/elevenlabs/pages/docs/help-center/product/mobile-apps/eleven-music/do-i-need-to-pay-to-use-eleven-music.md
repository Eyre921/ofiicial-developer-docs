# Do I need to pay to use ElevenMusic?

We offer two subscription plans for ElevenMusic, including a free plan. 

The free plan allows you to generate 5 tracks per day, while the Pro subscription offers 400 tracks per month, and a range of other benefits:

* Increased storage
* Styles packs
* Stem separation
* HQ WAV downloads (10 per month)
* Generate cover art
* Song analytics
* Earn royalties

For full details and to upgrade to the Pro subscription, visit the ElevenMusic [website](https://elevenmusic.io/) or check the ElevenMusic [app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/elevenmusic-by-elevenlabs/id6755965224) (iOS only).
