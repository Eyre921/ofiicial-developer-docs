# How much does GenFM cost?

There is currently no cost for generating the text of your podcast. You will be charged the regular cost of converting the text to audio, and will be notified how many credits it will cost to convert the audio before you confirm the conversion. When you generate a podcast using Studio, you can edit the text before converting and can export the audio.
