# What is GenFM?

GenFM allows you to automatically turn any content - PDFs, articles, text, links, eBooks - into a dynamic podcast, featuring discussion with two lifelike AI co-hosts.

GenFM is available in [Studio](https://elevenlabs.io/app/studio) - for details, see [How do I use GenFM?](/docs/help-center/product/distribution-publishing/gen-fm/how-do-i-use-gen-fm)
