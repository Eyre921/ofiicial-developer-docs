# If I uninstall the ElevenReader app, will I still be charged?

Yes. Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. To avoid future charges, make sure to cancel your subscription through your app store or account settings before uninstalling.

For details on how to cancel your subscription, please see [How do I cancel my ElevenReader subscription?](/docs/help-center/product/distribution-publishing/eleven-reader/how-do-i-cancel-my-eleven-reader-subscription)
