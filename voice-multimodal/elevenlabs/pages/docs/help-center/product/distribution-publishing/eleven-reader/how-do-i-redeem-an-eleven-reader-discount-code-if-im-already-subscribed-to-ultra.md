# How do I redeem an ElevenReader discount code if I’m already subscribed to Ultra?

If you’ve received a discount code, you can enter it when purchasing an Ultra plan via the web: <a href="https://elevenreader.io/pricing">[https://elevenreader.io/pricing](https://elevenreader.io/pricing)</a> 

If you’re currently subscribed to Ultra through the App Store or Google Play, you’ll first need to cancel that subscription from those stores on your device before switching to a web subscription with a discount added. Unfortunately, Apple and Google don’t support overlapping subscriptions or applying discounts to a current subscription. Once your current subscription has ended, you can purchase an Ultra plan via the web and apply your discount code at checkout. 

Follow these steps:

Cancel your existing Ultra subscription from the App Store or Google Play

Wait for your current subscription to expire, and your plan returns to Free

Purchase a new subscription using your discount code on{" "}
<a href="http://elevenreader.io">elevenreader.io</a>

You can manage your subscription purchased from the web anytime from your account: <a href="https://elevenreader.io/reader/account">[https://elevenreader.io/reader/account](https://elevenreader.io/reader/account)</a>
