# What is ElevenReader?

ElevenReader is a powerful tool that enables users to convert text into natural, expressive speech. Whether you're reading articles, EPUBs, PDFs, or any form of text, this app is designed to enhance your reading experience by providing high-quality, human-like voices in 32 languages.

Leveraging ElevenLabs' most advanced Text to Speech (TTS) model, ElevenReader places the cutting-edge technology of ElevenLabs right in your pocket.
