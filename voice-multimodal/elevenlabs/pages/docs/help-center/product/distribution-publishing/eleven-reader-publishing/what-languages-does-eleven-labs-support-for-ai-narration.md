# What languages does ElevenLabs support for AI narration?

ElevenLabs supports over 30 languages, including English (US, UK, Australian, Canadian), Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, and many others. This enables authors to reach a global audience with diverse languages and accents. Please see a full list of supported languages here: [Supported Languages.](/docs/help-center/other/what-languages-do-you-support)

While our audio model supports multiple languages, the beta version of the ElevenReader Publishing program currently accepts content only in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, German or French. However, we plan to expand support for additional languages in the future.
