# What is the best way to increase discoverability of my content?

Here are some effective methods to boost the discoverability of your content:

Share your book with your audience using the unique sharing link.

Ensure your metadata is clean, accurate, and descriptive to improve searchability.

Select a high-resolution cover art design with a clear, readable title.
