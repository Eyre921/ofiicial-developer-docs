# What is ElevenLabs and the ElevenReader App?

ElevenLabs is an Audio AI research and deployment company. Our mission is to make content universally accessible in any language and in any voice. Our research team develops advanced AI audio models that generate realistic, versatile and contextually-aware speech, sound effects, and voices across 32 languages. Access the ElevenLabs platform here: [https://elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io)

ElevenReader is a text-to-audio and audiobook app for iOS, Android, and web. It is widely available in countries and on devices where supported in 32 languages. Choose from 200,000+ premium audiobooks and eBooks — or bring your own content. Upload any PDF, article, EPUB, or document and hear it in stunningly natural voices you choose from a library of 1,000+ options across languages. Download free from the App Store or Google Play, or get started on the web at [ElevenReader.io](http://ElevenReader.io).
