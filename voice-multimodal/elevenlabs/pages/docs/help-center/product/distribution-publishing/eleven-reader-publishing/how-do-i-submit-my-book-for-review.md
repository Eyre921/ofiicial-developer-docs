# How do I submit my book for review?

Once you have completed all the steps in the publishing process, you can submit your book for review. This will update the status of your book in the “Bookshelf” view on [ElevenReader Publishing](https://elevenreader.io/app/publishing) from ‘Draft’ to ‘Pending Review.’
