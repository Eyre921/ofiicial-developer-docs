# Can I remove my content from ElevenReader?

Yes, you can remove content via your publishing account. Please see [How can I delete/unlist a book that has been published](/docs/help-center/product/distribution-publishing/elevenreader-publishing/how-can-i-delete-unlist-a-book-that-has-been-published) for detailed instructions. Purchased content will remain accessible to users in their personal libraries without limitation, while free content will be available for one month after removal.
