# What is My Voices?

[My Voices](https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-lab) is our one stop shop for voice cloning and creating new voices.

All users can generate synthetic voices using [Voice Design](/docs/product-guides/voices/voice-design). These voices can't be shared with other users.

On the Starter plan and above, you can use [Instant Voice Cloning](/docs/voices/voice-lab/instant-voice-cloning) to create cloned voices with just less than 2 minutes of training audio. Voices created using Instant Voice Cloning are instantly available for use. You must have permission to clone the voice, and these voices can't be shared.

On the Creator plan and above, you can use [Professional Voice Cloning](/docs/voices/voice-lab/professional-voice-cloning) to create a realistic model of your own voice. This process takes longer, as it involves training a dedicated model on a large set of voice data. You can share your PVC privately with a list of individuals that you specify. You can also share it via a sharing link, or make it available publicly in the [Voice Library](https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-library).

For more information on how to share your voices, please see [How do I share a voice?](/docs/help-center/product/voices/my-voices/how-do-i-share-a-voice)
