# Who has access to my voices?

Unless you explicitly share a voice, the voices you’ve created are **only accessible through your account.**

[Professional Voice Clones](/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/professional-voice-cloning) can be shared:

* Privately with members of your workspace
* Externally with other ElevenLabs users via a sharing link
* Publicly with the community via the [Voice Library](https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-library)

If you share a Professional Voice Clone with the Voice Library, you can [earn money via Voice Actor Payouts](/docs/help-center/product/monetization-business/payouts/what-are-voice-actor-payouts) when paid users generate audio with your voice. Voice sharing options and metrics for Professional Voice Clones are only available on the Creator plan or above.

Instant Voice Clones and voices created with [Voice Design](/docs/product-guides/voices/voice-design) can only be shared with members of your workspace. They cannot be shared outside your workspace or published to the Voice Library.

For more details, see [How do I share a voice?](/docs/help-center/product/voices/my-voices/how-do-i-share-a-voice)
