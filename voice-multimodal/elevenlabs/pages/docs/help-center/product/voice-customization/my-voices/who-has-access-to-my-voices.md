# Who has access to my voices?

Unless you explicitly share a voice with another account or to the Voice Library, the voices you've created are <strong>only accessible through your account.</strong>

Professional Voice Clones can be shared with other ElevenLabs users. You can share individual voices with specific friends and colleagues via a sharing link then whitelisting their account email address. You can also share Professional Voice Clones with the community via the Voice Library. 

If you share your Professional Voice Clone with the Voice Library, you have the option to receive financial rewards when someone uses your voice. Voice sharing options and metrics for Professional Voice Clones are only available if you're on the Creator plan or above.

It's not possible to share voices created by Instant Voice Cloning or Voice Design.

To find out more about sharing, you can read this article: [How do I share a voice?](/docs/help-center/product/voice-customization/my-voices/how-do-i-share-a-voice)
