# Why can't I access my voices via third-party services?

If you've linked your ElevenLabs account with a third-party service, but are not able to access the voices that you have saved to [My Voices](https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-lab), it's likely that there is an error with your API key.

Your API key is what links the third-party service with your ElevenLabs account. You can access your API keys from the [Developers page](https://elevenlabs.io/app/developers), under the API Keys tab. 

For more details, please see our article, [How to authorize yourself using your xi-api-key?](/docs/help-center/technical/how-do-i-authorize-myself-using-an-api-key)
