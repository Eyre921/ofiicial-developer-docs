# How do I share a voice?

We only allow the sharing of professional cloned voices verified by a human. Instant Voice Clones, synthetic voices created using [Voice Design](/docs/product-guides/voices/voice-design), AI-generated or AI-enhanced voices cannot be shared in the Voice Library.

When sharing a [Professional Voice Clone](/docs/voices/voice-lab/professional-voice-cloning) of your voice, you have full control over it's shared. If you decide to stop sharing it, anyone who has saved your voice will also lose access. If you shared your voice with the Voice Library and agreed a notice period, anyone who saved your voice will retain access for the duration of the notice period, provided they don't remove your voice.

* You can share your voice to individuals by whitelisting them.
* You can share your voice to the [Voice Library](/docs/product-guides/voices/voice-library) and <strong>earn money via our</strong><a href="/docs/help-center/product/monetization-business/payouts/what-are-voice-actor-payouts"><strong>Voice Actor Payouts</strong></a>! More on this further down.

<strong>NOTE</strong>: If you share your voice, either privately or with the Voice Library, you
won't be able to edit your voice in the future. You'll need to delete the voice and create a new
voice, which can't be done while your voice is shared with the Voice Library, or during an active
notice period. 

Here's how to share your voice with the Voice Library:

1. In [My Voices,](https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-lab) find your voice and click <strong>More actions</strong> (three dots), then select <strong>Share voice</strong>.

<img src="https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/article_attachments/35962339415185" alt="" />

2. In the pop-up, enable the Sharing toggle.

3. For private sharing, copy the sharing link. This will allow other users to save your voice to their account.

You can restrict access to specific users by adding emails to the <strong>Allowlist</strong>. If this is left blank, all users with the link will be able to access your voice.

4. To share publicly, enable <strong>Publish to the Voice Library</strong>. This doesn’t make your voice automatically discoverable.

<img src="https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/article_attachments/35962377669265" alt="" />

5. Before proceeding with the sharing process, you’ll have a number of options including setting a notice period and enabling Live Moderation. Please see the [Voice Library Addendum](https://elevenlabs.io/vla) to our [Terms of Service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) for more information about these options, as well as these Help Center articles:

* [What is a notice period?](/docs/help-center/product/voice-customization/voice-library/what-is-a-notice-period)
* [What is Live Moderation?](/docs/help-center/product/voice-customization/voice-library/what-is-live-moderation)

<img src="https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/article_attachments/35962339419281" alt="" />

You also have the option to select a custom voice preview. Any generations you’ve made of 70-150 characters will be available to select. If you don’t see any options in the selection menu, there are no eligible generations available. For more details on this, please see [How do I add a custom preview for my shared voice?](/docs/help-center/product/voice-customization/voice-library/how-do-i-add-a-custom-preview-for-my-shared-voice)

6. Enter a name and description for your voice. Make sure the name you give your voice follows our naming guidelines, which are available [here.](/docs/product-guides/voices/voice-library#sharing-a-professional-voice-clone)

7. Set labels (language, accent, gender, age, use case, tone, and style) to help others find your voice.

8. Review and accept the [Voice Library Addendum](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use#VLA) to our [Terms of Service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) and provide the required consents and confirmations. Please do this carefully and ensure you fully understand our service before sharing. If you have any questions at this stage, you can reach out to us at [legal@elevenlabs.io.](mailto:legal@elevenlabs.io) 

9. After submission, your voice will be reviewed by our team. If minor adjustments are needed, we may make these for you. Your request to share your voice may be declined if it doesn’t meet our guidelines, and repeated uploads that consistently violate our guidelines may lead to restrictions on uploading and sharing voices.

We currently do not have an estimate for the review time, as it depends on the queue.

You can view metrics for your voice after sharing it by clicking <strong>More options</strong> (three dots) for your voice, and selecting <strong>View metrics</strong>. Voice sharing options and metrics are only available if you are on the Creator plan or above.
