# Saturday Sessions

## <strong>General</strong>

<strong>
  What is Saturday Session?
</strong>

On Saturday, April 11, 2026, every audio and music generation in ElevenCreative costs 11x fewer credits. The promotion runs for 24 hours, from 00:00 to 23:59 UTC.

<strong>
  Who is eligible?
</strong>

Everyone on ElevenCreative across all self-serve plans: Free, Starter, Creator, Pro, Scale, and Business. Enterprise plans are not included.

<strong>
  What time does it start and end in my time zone?
</strong>

The promotion runs from 00:00 to 23:59 UTC on April 11. Conversions:

Pacific Time: 5:00 PM Friday, April 10 to 4:59 PM Saturday, April 11

Eastern Time: 8:00 PM Friday, April 10 to 7:59 PM Saturday, April 11

BST (London): 1:00 AM Saturday, April 11 to 12:59 AM Sunday, April 12

CEST (Berlin): 2:00 AM Saturday, April 11 to 1:59 AM Sunday, April 12

IST (India): 5:30 AM Saturday, April 11 to 5:29 AM Sunday, April 12

JST (Tokyo): 9:00 AM Saturday, April 11 to 8:59 AM Sunday, April 12

AEST (Sydney): 10:00 AM Saturday, April 11 to 9:59 AM Sunday, April 12

## <strong>What’s Included</strong>

<strong>
  Which products are included?
</strong>

Text to Speech, Studio, Music, Sound Effects, Audiobooks, and Voice generation. These are all ElevenLabs-owned audio models.

<strong>
  Is Image & Video generation included?
</strong>

No. This Saturday Session applies to audio and music models only. Image and video generation is not included in this promotion.

<strong>
  Do I need to opt in or enter a code?
</strong>

No. The reduced pricing applies automatically for everyone on ElevenCreative during the 24-hour window on April 11.

## <strong>Pricing & Credits</strong>

<strong>
  How does the 11x work exactly?
</strong>

Every eligible generation costs 11x fewer credits than it normally would. If a generation normally costs 110 credits, it will cost 10 credits on April 11. The discount is applied at the point of generation.

<strong>
  Does this apply to my existing credits or do I get bonus credits?
</strong>

This applies to your existing credit balance. You are not receiving additional credits. Each generation simply costs less on April 11. Your credits go further.
