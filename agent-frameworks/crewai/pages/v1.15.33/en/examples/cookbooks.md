## Quickstarts & Demos

> **[Collaboration](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI-quickstarts/blob/main/Collaboration/crewai_collaboration.ipynb)**
>
> Coordinate multiple agents on shared tasks. Includes notebook with end-to-end collaboration pattern.

> **[Planning](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI-quickstarts/blob/main/Planning/crewai_planning.ipynb)**
>
