Standalone end-to-end courses showing how to use Fireworks for real-world use cases across fine-tuning, RAG, agents, and multi-modal inference.

> **[Text to SQL](/examples/text-to-sql)**
>
> Learn how to use Fireworks to train a model to convert natural language to SQL queries.

> **[Reward Hacking](/examples/reward-hacking)**
>
> Learn how to build reinforcement learning systems that avoid reward hacking.

> **[Knowledge Distillation](/examples/knowledge-distillation)**
>
> Learn to distill the knowledge of large AI models into efficient, deployable alternatives.
