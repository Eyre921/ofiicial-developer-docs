---
title: "Delete an A/B experiment"
source: https://docs.together.ai/reference/dmi/ab-experiments-delete
path: reference/dmi/ab-experiments-delete
---

openapi.yaml DELETE /projects/{projectId}/endpoints/{endpointId}/abExperiments/{id}
Deletes an A/B experiment and removes its managed traffic split. The deployments themselves are not deleted.
