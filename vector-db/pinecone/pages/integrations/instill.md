Wire Pinecone into Instill AI no-code pipelines to upsert, query, and power RAG agents, chatbots, and knowledge bases with vector similarity.

Instill AI builds tools for data, models, and pipeline orchestration. Its flagship source-available product, Instill Core, is a no-code and low-code platform for developing, deploying, and managing AI workflows. Instill Core integrates AI models and data sources so you can build AI applications without extensive technical expertise, for use cases that range from predictive analytics and autonomous AI agents to enterprise private knowledge bases and AI assistants.

The Pinecone integration with Instill adds Pinecone vector upsert and query tasks to your AI pipelines. To configure the Pinecone component in Instill Core, provide your API key and base URL. You can then query for similar vectors to retrieve the most relevant results, with metadata and similarity scores, or upsert new vectors to keep your datasets up to date. This lets you add knowledge to LLMs through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for autonomous agents, chatbots, question-answering systems, and multi-agent systems.
