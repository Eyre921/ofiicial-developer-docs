Use HoneyHive with Pinecone to capture OpenTelemetry traces of SDK calls and visualize spans for observability and evaluation of RAG pipelines.

[HoneyHive](https://www.honeyhive.ai/) is an observability and evaluation platform that helps you visualize the execution traces and spans of your Pinecone application. The HoneyHive Python and TypeScript SDKs instrument Pinecone SDK functions and API calls, giving you a breakdown of your pipeline. HoneyHive collects the OpenTelemetry (OTel) traces and spans and exports them to honeyhive.ai, where they're processed.
