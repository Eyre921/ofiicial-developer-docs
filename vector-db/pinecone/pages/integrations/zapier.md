Use Zapier to connect Pinecone to 6,000+ apps, trigger workflows when an index changes, and add data or run searches without writing code.

[Zapier](https://zapier.com/) lets you connect Pinecone with thousands of popular apps and automate your work without writing code.

With this integration, Pinecone can trigger workflows when your index status changes, and other apps can command Pinecone to perform actions. This means you can automatically add or remove data, run searches, or manage indexes based on events happening in your other tools.

For example, you might use this integration to automatically add spreadsheet entries to your Pinecone index, or build a bot that searches for answers and posts them directly to your team chat. The integration brings Pinecone search and data operations into your existing workflows.
