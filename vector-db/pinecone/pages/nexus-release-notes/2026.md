Pinecone Nexus release notes for 2026.

## August 2026

<Update label="2026-08-06">
  ### Nexus launch

  Pinecone Nexus, the knowledge engine for agents, is now generally available. Point Nexus at your sources, curate them into a context, and query that context to get grounded, cited answers. Get started in the [Nexus quickstart](/guides/nexus/quickstart).
</Update>
