Compare which Pinecone plans include support tickets and first-response SLAs, see how SLA time is counted, and change your support plan.

## Support plans

Pinecone has four support plans: Free, Developer, Pro, and Premium. Your support plan determines whether your tickets get [first-response SLAs](#first-response-slas). The Free support plan lets you open tickets but has no SLAs.

Which support plans you can use depends on your organization's [billing plan](/admin/billing/upgrade-billing-plan):

| Billing plan | Support tickets | Included support plan | Support plans you can add |
| - | - | - | - |
| Starter | No | None | None |
| Builder | Yes | Free | Developer or Pro |
| Standard | Yes | Free, or Pro with an annual commit | Developer or Pro |
| Enterprise | Yes | Pro, or Premium with an annual commit | Premium, by [contacting sales](https://www.pinecone.io/contact/?contact_form_inquiry_type=Enterprise+Pricing) |

On Starter, you can still use the support chatbot and the community, as described in [Get help](/admin/support/contact-support). For pricing and the other features of each support plan, see [support pricing](https://www.pinecone.io/pricing/?plans=support).

## First-response SLAs

SLAs depend on your support plan and the ticket's [severity](/admin/support/contact-support#choose-a-severity).

| Severity | Developer | Pro | Premium |
| - | - | - | - |
| Sev-1 | 8 business hours | 2 hours, 24/7 | 30 minutes, 24/7 |
| Sev-2 | 12 business hours | 4 business hours | 2 business hours |
| Sev-3 | 2 business days | 12 business hours | 8 business hours |
| Sev-4 | 3 business days | 2 business days | 12 business hours |

You can open a ticket at any time. SLA times marked 24/7 run around the clock. Business hours and business days count only Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays.

## Change your support plan

Before you can add a paid support plan, [upgrade your billing plan](/admin/billing/upgrade-billing-plan) to Builder, Standard, or Enterprise. You must be an [organization owner or billing admin](/admin/organizations/understanding-organizations#organization-roles) to change the support plan.

1. Go to [**Settings > Support > Plan options**](https://app.pinecone.io/organizations/-/settings/support/plans).
2. Select the plan you want.
