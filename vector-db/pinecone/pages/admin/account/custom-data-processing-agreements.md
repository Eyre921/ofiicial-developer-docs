Request a custom data processing agreement (DPA) with Pinecone if your team requires terms beyond the standard agreement available on the Pinecone website.

To request a data processing agreement (DPA) with Pinecone, fill out the form in the Pinecone [Security Center](https://security.pinecone.io/). You'll need to request access first. Click **Get Access** on that page and enter your information. You should receive a link with further instructions shortly.
